The Willow Book homepage image asset pack

These PNGs are cropped icons and decorative sprigs used on the live marketing
site (homepage features, occasion tiles, footer accents, and hero preview).

Files ending in "_transparent.png" use best-effort background removal and may
need minor cleanup before production use.

For production, the line-art icons should ideally be recreated as SVGs so they
remain crisp at all sizes.
